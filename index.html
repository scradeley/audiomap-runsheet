  • auto-created, per-source — one tuning per box

New v2 · Groups

One aligned-sum tuning across a group. FOH: quick measures → parallel tuning on the master bus. Or per-section: tops / fills / subs.

Default — per-box (best result)

  • handles L-vs-R room differences
  • corrects at the source → max power per box, before the room varies it

How it's built

  • power average over coverage → magnitude curve to correct
  • inverse = target correction
  • algo adapts in real time to your DSP:
    • # bands IIR/FIR min/lin phase band-limited shelves parallel/serial
  • 4 bands → best fit · 8/12/24 → approaches flat
  • every band visible — in-app or export
